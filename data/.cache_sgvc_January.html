Open-Source Unit Testing for LLM Applications

Seed

AI & SaaS

Global

Visit website

As large language models (LLMs) become integral to enterprise solutions, ensuring their reliability and performance is paramount. Traditional evaluation methods often fall short in providing the necessary insights for high-stakes deployments.

Confident AI addresses this challenge with DeepEval, an open-source framework designed for rigorous LLM evaluation, which was also built by the team. DeepEval offers a suite of customizable metrics, enabling developers to unit-test LLM applications effectively. Its capabilities include benchmarking, regression testing, and red-teaming, facilitating the identification and mitigation of potential issues before deployment.

‍

With over 500,000 monthly downloads and integration into CI/CD pipelines of leading enterprises like BCG, AstraZeneca, AXA, and Microsoft, Confident AI has become a trusted tool in the AI development community. By providing a robust evaluation infrastructure, Confident AI empowers teams to build more reliable, efficient, and trustworthy LLM-powered applications.
