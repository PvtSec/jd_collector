Founders
First

Our founders come from all walks of life with ambitions to transform millions of lives through technology. We understand the importance of a founder and their role as the foundation of any good company. From ideating to designing the vision of the company, a founder plays a crucial role in using technology as an anchor to drive impact.

As investors and founders ourselves, we deeply value founders. We only make a small number of investments each year in great founders we believe in. This is why we will put our all in any founder we invest in. We also believe in the need to earn the right to invest.
