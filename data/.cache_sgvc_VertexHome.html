Meet our team

As an early stage investor and one of Southeast Asia's oldest venture capital funds, we have invested in more than 90 startups at their seed, series A/B round. In addition to capital, we pride ourselves on adding value to our founders by working closely with them and helping them across introductions to valuable contacts, hiring key management, go-to-market strategies, fundraising and more. We are pleased that our founders have been doing well by growing quickly, achieving unicorn status or becoming public companies.

Driving Impact through Investments

As an early stage, sector agnostic VC focusing on Southeast Asia and India, our investment thesis is anchored around the massive growth potential of the digital economy in the SE Asia and India region. Given the deep emerging market characteristics, many of our portfolio companies are well positioned to bring about significant impact to their stakeholders as they grow, particularly in accelerating economic empowerment, MSME enablement,  promoting inclusiveness and contributing towards climate action across the region.

    No consideration of adverse impacts of investment decisions on sustainability factors in accordance with Article 4(1)(b) Regulation (EU) 2019/2088 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 27 November 2019 on sustainability-related disclosures in the financial services sector (SFDR))


    For the purpose of Article 4(1)(b) of the SFDR, Vertex Ventures SEA Management Pte. Ltd. ("Vertex Ventures SEA"), as a financial market participant, does not consider the adverse impacts of investment decisions on all of the sustainability factors as referred to in Article 4(1)(a) of the SFDR and does not make the disclosures as described in Article 4(1)(a) of the SFDR. The principal adverse impacts within the meaning of Article 4(1)(a) of the SFDR do not have sufficient relevance on Vertex Ventures SEA's investments, due to the size of Vertex Ventures SEA's investments and the structure of Vertex Ventures SEA's investment lines. Additionally, there is no sufficient data available for Vertex Ventures SEA to measure the principal adverse impacts within the meaning of Article 4(1)(a) of the SFDR. Vertex Ventures SEA will review its position on a regular basis, taking into account the evolution of the applicable rules.

    In addition to Southeast Asia and India, the Vertex Global Network is comprised of affiliates in China, Israel, Japan, and the US.
